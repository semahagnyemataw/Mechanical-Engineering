About us

    The Department of Mechanical Engineering is committed to excellence in engineering education, scientific research, technological innovation, and community service. The department equips students with the theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and problem-solving abilities required to address industrial and societal challenges.

    Its academic and research activities cover major areas of mechanical engineering, including Thermal and Energy Engineering, Manufacturing and Production Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Mechanical Design, and Mechatronics and Control Engineering.

    Through competency-based education, practical laboratory training, applied research, industry collaboration, and technology transfer, the department strives to produce competent, innovative, and socially responsible engineers who contribute to sustainable industrial development and national economic growth.

Main Streams

  • Manufacturing Engineering:

      • It is about Manufacturing processes, materials, industrial production, automation, and quality control

  • Automotive Engineering:

      • It is about Design, operation, maintenance, and performance of motor vehicles, engines, and vehicle systems.

  • Thermal Engineering:

      It is about Thermodynamics, heat transfer, fluid mechanics, power generation, renewable energy, refrigeration, and air conditioning.

  • Design Engineering:

      It is about Machine design, solid mechanics, vibration, stress analysis, and mechanical system development.

Major Concerns

Teaching and Academic Programs

Deliver quality undergraduate and postgraduate education, develop curricula, conduct practical laboratory training, supervise student projects, and develop competent Mechanical Engineers.

Research, Innovation, and Technology Development

Conduct scientific and applied research in manufacturing, automotive engineering, thermal and energy systems, and mechanical design; publish research findings and develop practical engineering solutions.

Industry Linkage and Community Service

Establish partnerships with industries and public institutions, organize internships, address community engineering problems, transfer technology, and provide professional and technical training.

Laboratory Development, Quality Assurance, and Institutional Capacity Building

Maintain and modernize laboratories and workshops, ensure equipment safety, strengthen staff development, improve academic quality, manage resources effectively, and pursue accreditation and continuous improvement.

Engineering Consultancy and Professional Services

Provide expert services in mechanical design, manufacturing, automotive systems, HVAC and refrigeration, energy efficiency, equipment selection, maintenance, engineering simulation, feasibility studies, and technical auditing within the department's capabilities.
